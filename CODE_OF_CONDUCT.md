# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want PySolveHub to be a welcoming, respectful place for people with different experience levels and backgrounds.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Welcome questions from beginners.
- Explain feedback clearly and kindly.
- Focus criticism on code and ideas, not people.
- Respect different opinions and learning speeds.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, discrimination, or threats.
- Trolling, personal attacks, or deliberate disruption.
- Publishing someone else's private information without permission.
- Repeatedly making the community hostile or unsafe.

## Maintainer responsibility

Project maintainers may edit, reject, or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, contact the repository owner through the contact method listed on the GitHub profile. Please include relevant links or context. Reports will be handled as respectfully and confidentially as possible.
